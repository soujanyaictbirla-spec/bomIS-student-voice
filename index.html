How to use:

1. Enter Student Name.
2. Select Grade.
3. Select English or Telugu.
4. Click Start Recording.
5. Speak into the laptop microphone or headset.
6. Speech will appear as text.
7. Click Stop.
8. Click Download Audio to save the recording.
9. Text is automatically sent to Google Sheet.
