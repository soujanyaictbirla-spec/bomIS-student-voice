BOMIS Student Voice Recorder

Two-Person Student Conversation
Ready
How to use:
  1. Enter the student's name.
  2. Select the grade and date.
  3. Select Person 1.
  4. Click Start and speak.
  5. Click Stop.
  6. Select Person 2.
  7. Click Start and let Person 2 speak.
  8. Click Stop.
  9. Click Save.
For the same student, the conversation will be kept in the same Google Sheet row.
